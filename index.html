About

From North Dakota to Curaçao

Paris Smith is one of the most respected leaders in the global gaming industry, with a career spanning more than three decades. Her path from North Dakota to a Caribbean island at the center of the world's online gaming business is a story of betting on herself and winning. She began in 1995 at WWTS in Antigua, rising to Executive Vice President and then Chief Operating Officer of its parent company, Betcorp, before joining Pinnacle Sports in 2006 as COO. She was named Chief Executive Officer in 2008.

Over 17 years leading Pinnacle, Paris transformed the Curaçao-based bookmaker into one of the world's most admired sportsbook brands, renowned for its low-margin, gimmick-free model and trusted by sharp bettors across the globe. Under her leadership, Pinnacle pioneered industry firsts, including accepting the world's first esports bets in 2010. Along the way she advised governments on building regulatory frameworks for online betting.

In 2023, Paris stepped back from day-to-day operations into an advisory role and began a new chapter as an investor. Today she invests and advises through her firm Life Winnings, co-founded the startup incubator Defy the Odds, and is a partner in BettingStartups Capital, an early-stage fund for real-money gaming startups. She remains a sought-after voice at industry events worldwide, from ICE and SBC Summit to iGaming NEXT.

In 2025 Paris became the first woman inducted into the Sports Gambling Hall of Fame, eight years after joining the SBC Sports Betting Hall of Fame. In 2026, iGaming Business named her one of the industry's Most Influential Women.

Paris lives in Curaçao with her dog, Patience. Proof that even after thirty years in one of the world's fastest-moving industries, the best things are worth waiting for.
